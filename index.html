Questions


Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, on most modern phones with NFC turned on. If a customer's phone has no NFC, Version 2 has a QR code beside the NFC area that opens the same link.

What is the difference between Version 1 and Version 2?

Version 1 is NFC tap only. Version 2 has the NFC tap area on the left and a QR code on the right, both on the same face of the card.

Can I upload my own design?

Yes. Use the custom design preview to see your artwork on the card. We confirm the final design with you in chat before we print.

Do customers need an app?

No. The link opens in their browser.

Will it get me more 5-star reviews?

We cannot promise that. It makes leaving a review easier. Your service decides the stars.

Can I change the link later?

Yes. Message us and we will reprogram compatible cards.

Can I mix cards in one order?

Yes. Any two cards in one order save ₱299, even if they are different.

When will it arrive?

We confirm the exact date when we confirm your order. It depends on payment and the courier.

I have several branches. Can I order in bulk?

Yes. Write the numbers in your order notes and we will send a quote.
