What we collect

We only collect what you type into our order form or send us in a message:

  • Your name
  • Your business name
  • Your mobile number or Messenger contact
  • Your delivery address
  • The pack you chose, the total, and any coupon code you used
  • Notes you add, such as your Google review link or branding details
  • Artwork files and design settings, if you choose to send us a custom design

We do not ask for your passwords, government ID numbers or card numbers through this website.

How the order form works

This website does not store your order on its own server. When you press the send button, your browser opens Facebook Messenger with your order details filled into a message addressed to our page. The message is only sent when you press send inside Messenger. Messenger is operated by Meta, which handles that message under its own privacy policy.

Custom design preview

When you use the design preview tool, your image is opened only inside your own browser to draw the preview. It is not uploaded to this website and we do not receive it. If you want us to print a design, you attach the file yourself in a Messenger chat with us. Meta handles that message under its own privacy policy.

Why we use your information

  • To confirm, prepare, program and deliver your order
  • To contact you about payment, delivery and support
  • To honor our replace or refund guarantee
  • To keep records of sales as required by law

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising by third parties.

Who we share it with

Only as needed to complete your order:

  • Couriers, so they can deliver to your address
  • Payment providers or banks, if you pay through them
  • Meta, because orders are sent through Messenger
  • Government agencies, if the law requires it

Cookies, analytics and fonts

This website does not use cookies, analytics tools or browser storage.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google servers to fetch them, and Google may receive your IP address as part of that request.

How long we keep it

We keep order information for 3 years for warranty, accounting and legal purposes, then delete it.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed
  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Object to processing or withdraw your consent
  • Ask us to delete or block your data, subject to our legal record-keeping duties
  • Be indemnified for damages caused by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or unauthorized use of your data
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above.

Security

We keep order details in our own accounts and limit access to the people who need them to fulfill your order. No method of storing or sending data is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

Our products are for businesses. This website is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
